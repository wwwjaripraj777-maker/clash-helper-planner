# Detection Checklist (For Personal Review Only)

This checklist is intended to help you review recorded gameplay sessions and decide whether any automated or suspicious activity may have occurred. It is strictly a manual/read-only tool: it does not automate or control any other app.

How to use
- Record your session using the app's recorder and take screenshots.
- Export or view session timestamps and intervals.
- Use this checklist to manually review patterns — the app may offer a simple analyzer that flags items but ALWAYS review manually.

Checklist items (non-technical):
- Timing Regularity: Are actions occurring with extremely regular intervals (e.g., exact same interval repeatedly)?
- Reaction Time: Are actions faster than a reasonable human reaction (sub-100ms repetitively)?
- Repeated Sequences: Do you notice identical action sequences used many times in a row?
- Multi-account Patterns: Are many accounts performing similar actions from the same device/IP?
- Headless Runs: Is there evidence the app ran while device was idle/off-screen? (check logs)
- External Tools: Are there logs or files that indicate control from an external program or remote server?

If you suspect any violation:
- Stop using any third-party automation tools immediately.
- Change your account password and enable two-factor authentication if available.
- Review the game's Terms of Service and consider contacting official support if you believe your account was compromised.

Important: This checklist is for awareness and manual review only. It intentionally avoids deep technical analyses that could be repurposed to build evasion tools.
